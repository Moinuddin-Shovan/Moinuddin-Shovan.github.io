At Fiftytwo Digital I work on an omni-channel retail platform used by grocery, DIY, beauty and paint retailers across Scandinavia, Germany and Poland. Most of my time goes to the parts of the till that meet hardware and money: payment terminal drivers, fiscal printers, receipt and journal output, and the tender logic behind returns and corrections.

This is the kind of software nobody notices until it fails at a busy checkout. So I care about the unglamorous parts: reproducing a customer's bug exactly before touching it, making payment flows recover when a terminal drops out mid-transaction, and leaving an automated test behind so the same bug stays fixed.

The systems I maintain handle more than 3 million transactions a day. I've closed over 300 tickets, from critical production incidents to spikes and quotations for new features, and I work closely with hardware vendors when a problem sits between our code and theirs. Day to day I'm part of an Agile team split between Denmark and Bangladesh, and I write code test-first where I can.

Before retail, I built Android and Flutter apps with on-device machine learning, and my thesis was a computer-vision system for detecting patient falls. I still follow that world closely: AI automation, MLOps and LLMOps, computer vision and security.

What I work on

A selection of my work at Fiftytwo Digital, grouped by area. Customer names are left out.

Card payments & EFT drivers

Verifone P400 and P630, Lane3000, payment SDKs

  • Made the payment flow recover when a card terminal aborts a card-info request mid-transaction, instead of leaving the till stuck.
  • Investigated and fixed broken reconnection after dropped terminal connections, including working with the vendor on whether their protocol needed an acknowledgement, and handled driver failures cleanly on the till side.
  • Fixed split payments and returns on the Verifone P630, cashback on Verifone terminals, and a terminal re-prompting for an already cancelled amount.
  • Resolved a double-payment issue and message-ordering bugs on Lane3000, and stopped terminal status updates from flooding the trace log.
  • Added automatic log rotation and cleanup to the payment SDK integration, plus a debug-output mode so vendors can diagnose issues from the field.
  • Corrected a protocol bug where optional JSON data was sent in the wrong terminal command.

Fiscal printing in Poland

POSNET thermal fiscal printers, KSeF e-invoicing

  • Built and maintained fiscal printer support for a paint retailer's Polish stores: sales, parked, cancelled and return receipts, and customers who are exempt from fiscalisation.
  • Implemented manual returns tied to KSeF, Poland's national e-invoicing system, across the POS UI and transaction records.
  • Fixed missing special characters, missing employee IDs and incorrect return receipts on fiscal and non-fiscal prints.

Receipts, returns & tender logic

Correction receipts, discounts, gift cards, cash statements

  • Fixed several cases where staff, B2B or senior discounts were wrongly paid out as cash on correction and return receipts.
  • Tracked down order numbers and basket discounts that "leaked" from one receipt into the next until the till was restarted.
  • Fixed digital receipt totals, multi-gift-card receipts, missing logos on receipt copies, and Z-reports missing from the terminal after a cash statement.
  • Stopped a production incident where tills were stuck printing the same card payment confirmation in a loop.

Loyalty, identity & APIs

Membership clubs, script API, identity management

  • Designed and implemented how loyalty points are distributed across receipt lines, starting from a spike and delivering it in two parts.
  • Updated membership club integrations: new HTTP PATCH support, endpoint changes, and fewer redundant impersonation-token calls.
  • Exposed POS and receipt context to shared services through the till's script API.
  • Worked on upgrading the WSO2 identity and access management layer to its latest version.

Services & algorithms

Low-latency APIs, microservices, loyalty search

  • Designed and integrated low-latency APIs that connect back-office servers with the cash registers.
  • Built microservices in JavaScript and Python on PostgreSQL, deployed with Docker on AWS.
  • Helped implement tree-search algorithms that pick the best combination of offers across several loyalty programs.
  • Optimised C++ services and components on systems handling over 3 million transactions a day.

Quality & delivery

Automated tests, spikes, quotations, branch merges
